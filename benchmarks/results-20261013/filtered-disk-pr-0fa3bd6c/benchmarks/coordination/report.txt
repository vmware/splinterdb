Filtered predicates integrated with disk-format: controlled end-to-end comparison

Ordinary tests: same harness; fixed CPU/thread-id assignments; full validating scan at one thread; one-second query warmup; phase-gated counters. Main/disk/combined forward-reverse order. Source/libs/binaries/fixtures are pinned by SHA256. Throughput on this laptop is sensitive to thermal/frequency drift; compare cycles/instructions and I/O with wall rates, not wall rates alone. User cycles/ref-cycles is the relative frequency indicator; user cycles/task-clock also includes kernel time in its denominator and must not be interpreted as physical frequency for I/O workloads. Feature counters aggregate the four measured limits, excluding load/warmup/close.
